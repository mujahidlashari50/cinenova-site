How to Watch & Download

Simple steps for mobile and desktop.

▶ How to Watch Online

1

Choose a movie

Tap or click any poster on the home page or in a category. The movie details will open.

2

Press Watch Now

On the detail screen, tap the Watch Now button.

3

Play in the player

The video player opens. Use play, pause, volume, and fullscreen as needed.

Tip: If the player is slow to load, wait a moment or check your connection. You can go back to the site anytime.

⬇ How to Download

1

Open the movie

Open the poster of the movie you want to download.

2

Select quality

Choose 480p (smaller file), 720p, or 1080p (best HD) — whichever is available.

3

Start download

Tap the download button or link. Your browser will save the file to your Downloads folder.

Tip: Use Chrome or Firefox on mobile. In-app browsers (WhatsApp, Facebook) may block downloads — use “Open in Browser” instead.

🔍 Search & Request

1

Search

Use the search icon in the header and type the movie name.

2

Request a Movie

If a title is missing, use the Request a Movie form on the home page — enter the title and submit.
