DMCA Policy

CineNova respects intellectual property rights. If you are a copyright owner, please follow the process below.

📢 Copyright Complaints

If you believe that content available through CineNova infringes your copyright, please send a proper DMCA notice with the following information:

• Your full name and contact information
• Description of the copyrighted work
• Exact URL(s) of the allegedly infringing material
• A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized
• A statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate
• Your physical or electronic signature

⚡ Takedown Process

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will review it and take appropriate action, which may include removing or disabling access to the reported content. We aim to respond promptly to legitimate claims.

📌 Important Note

CineNova primarily indexes and links to content hosted on third-party servers. We do not host movie files on our own servers. Incomplete or invalid notices may delay processing.

✉️ How to Reach Us

Please use the Contact page or our WhatsApp channel to submit copyright-related requests. Include “DMCA” in the subject so we can prioritize it.
